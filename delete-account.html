DutyTrack – Delete Account

DutyTrack provides users with the option to permanently delete their account and associated data.

How to Delete Your Account

You can permanently delete your DutyTrack account directly from the app.

Open the app and go to:

HR & Profile → Profile & Account Settings → Delete Account

Follow the confirmation steps to permanently delete your account.

Data That Will Be Deleted

When you delete your account, the following account-related information will be permanently deleted, where applicable:

  • 12-digit User ID
  • User profile information
  • Attendance records
  • Wage and salary-related records
  • Other user-created account data
  • Associated Cloud Firestore data

Data Retention

Account-related data is deleted when the account deletion process is completed, except where retention is required by applicable law or for legitimate legal purposes.
